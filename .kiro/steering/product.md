# Product Steering — ChimeraForge

- **Core Mission**: Eliminate the gap between natural-language software specifications and formal verification by stitching together EARS spec compilation, Property-Based Testing (PBT), visual graph physics, and acoustic telemetry.
- **Target Persona**: AI-native engineers, Kiro IDE/CLI power users, and hackathon builders who want spec-driven development (`Spec -> PBT -> Live Artifact`) to feel visceral, fast, and unbreakable.
- **Design Aesthetic**: High-contrast Cyber-Gothic Frankenstein laboratory (`#090b10` obsidian background, `#10b981` emerald verification pulse, `#f59e0b` amber warning arcs, `#ef4444` crimson counterexample alerts, monospace telemetry). Never use generic purple-blue AI slop gradients.
